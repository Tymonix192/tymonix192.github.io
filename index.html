About

I'm an electrical engineering student at EPFL. Most of what I know I learned on student teams, where nobody hands you a spec — you find out what the car or the robot needs, design the board, and then answer for it when it doesn't work on the first try.

The work I keep coming back to sits where power, control and firmware meet: a motor controller is a magnetics problem, a real-time software problem and a thermal problem at once, and you can't fake your way through any of them. Lately I've been adding RF to that list.

I'd like to spend the next few years on hardware that has to survive something — a race, a drop, a launch.
